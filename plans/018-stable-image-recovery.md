## Why this matters

`PortfolioSanityImage` retries by appending `cb=1`, creating a unique CDN cache key and potentially a fresh Sanity transformation. It does not distinguish an actual 404 from a transient network interruption. Recovery should reuse cached transforms and fall back deterministically.

## Current state

`PortfolioSanityImage.tsx:19-29` appends `cb=<attempt>` once, then renders a neutral fallback.

## Steps

4. Add protection against stale callbacks after navigation.
